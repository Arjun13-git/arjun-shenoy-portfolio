abstract: "We compare a classical attention-based CNN with a hybrid model that passes compressed image features through a 4-qubit variational quantum circuit, using Galaxy Zoo images across three morphology classes. The classical model is more accurate on clean data; the hybrid model holds its accuracy as Gaussian noise is added. Grad-CAM visualisations are used to compare where each model focuses."
